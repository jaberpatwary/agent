Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Daily Star e-paper?

The day's newspaper, page by page: the Dhaka Edition (Front, News, City, International, Editorial, Opinion, Art&Ent, Sport, Back) and the Business and Lifestyle supplements. A new edition is made every night after 12:00 am from the previous day's news.

How do I read a story?

Click a story on the page. In Page and Article View it opens beside the page; in Page View it opens over the page. The arrows beside the story take you to the previous or next story.

How do I go to another page?

Click a page in the list on the left, choose it in the page box of the tool bar, or use the blue arrows beside the page. On a keyboard, the left and right arrow keys turn the pages; on a phone, swipe.

How do I read an older edition?

Click the date in the tool bar and choose a day.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. In Text View, font-inc makes the story's text bigger with each click, then back to the start. Full View opens the story over the whole page.

Can the e-paper read a story aloud?

Yes. Open a story and click Listen; click Pause to stop. It uses your browser's own voice, so it works in most up-to-date browsers.

How do I save the edition as a PDF?

Click the download button in the tool bar, then choose Save as PDF in the print window. Every page of the edition is saved.

How do I share a page or a story?

Click the green share button on the page or the story, then choose Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp, or copy the link.

What is "Most Popular Article"?

The stories of the day's edition that readers opened most often. It is in the menu (☰).

Do I need to sign in?

No. The e-paper is free to read.
